# Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Shetkari Sanman Yojana – 2017

To provide relief to farmers affected by natural calamities and to bring them back into the institutional credit system, the Government of Maharashtra announced the **"Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Shetkari Sanman Yojana 2017"**. This historic decision has provided immense relief to lakhs of farmers who were pushed out of the banking system.

## ✨ Key Highlights and Historic Statistics

* **₹34,000 Crore Loan Waiver:** Under this scheme, loans of approximately **89 lakh farmers worth an estimated ₹34,000 crore were waived** — a historic decision.
* **Clean 7/12 Records:** Approximately **40 lakh farmers' 7/12 land records are now completely clean** due to this scheme.
* **Blanket Loan Waiver:** Farm loans up to ₹1.5 lakh (one and a half lakh) were waived outright.
* **Direct Bank Credit:** Farmers who regularly repaid their loans receive **up to ₹25,000 as incentive directly into their bank accounts**.
* **Return to Credit System:** Farmers who were pushed out of institutional credit due to defaulted loans now have a clear path to obtain fresh loans.

## 💰 Waiver Criteria and the Important Amendment of July 23, 2017

Initially, criteria were set through the Government Resolution of June 24, 2017, but a **major farmer-friendly amendment was made in the Cabinet meeting on July 23, 2017**:

* **Interest Waiver until July 31, 2017:** Under the revised decision, from the amount outstanding as of June 30, 2016, after deducting repayments made by the farmer, the remaining amount up to ₹1.5 lakh **including principal and interest as of July 31, 2017** is eligible for waiver. In short, the government also bore the burden of interest accrued until July 31, 2017.
* **One-Time Settlement (OTS):** For farmers whose dues exceed ₹1.5 lakh, if they deposit their share of the amount above ₹1.5 lakh in the bank, the government will provide the benefit of ₹1.5 lakh.

## 🏆 Incentive Benefits for Regular Repayers

* Farmers who fully repaid their 2015-16 crop loan by June 30, 2016, and their 2016-17 loan by June 30, 2017, will receive **25% of the 2015-16 loan amount or a maximum of ₹25,000** (whichever is less).
* The minimum incentive amount will be ₹15,000. If the repaid amount is less than ₹15,000, the full amount will be paid by the government.

## 📋 'Farmer Family' Criteria and Other Conditions

* The criterion of **"Farmer Family"** applies, which includes husband, wife, and children below 18 years.
* If there is a 'female farmer borrower' in the family, her loan will be considered on priority.
* No land-holding criteria (small/marginal farmer) has been applied; all eligible farmers receive the benefit outright.

## 🚫 Ineligibility: Who Cannot Avail the Benefit?

* Current/former Ministers, Members of Parliament (Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha), and Members of Legislative Assembly/Council.
* Members of Zilla Parishad and Municipal Corporation.
* Officers/employees of Central and State Government, Semi-Government organizations (excluding Class IV employees).
* Persons paying Income Tax from non-agricultural income.
* Persons receiving pension of more than ₹15,000 per month (excluding ex-servicemen).
* Business persons with gross income over ₹3 lakh registered for Service Tax / VAT.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**1. What is the maximum loan waiver under this scheme?**
Under this scheme, farm loans up to ₹1.5 lakh (including principal and interest) are waived outright.

**2. Will I get benefit if my outstanding is more than ₹1.5 lakh?**
Yes, the government has introduced a 'One Time Settlement (OTS)' for such farmers. If your loan exceeds ₹1.5 lakh and you deposit your share of the remaining amount above ₹1.5 lakh in the bank, the government will provide the ₹1.5 lakh loan waiver.

**3. Who receives the ₹25,000 incentive grant?**
Farmers who regularly and timely repay their crop loans receive an incentive grant of 25% of their loan amount or up to ₹25,000 directly in their bank account.

**4. Will this loan waiver actually clean farmers' 7/12 records?**
Yes, due to this historic decision and the blanket waiver up to ₹1.5 lakh, the 7/12 land records of approximately 40 lakh farmers will be completely cleaned.

**5. Is there any land-holding criteria in this scheme?**
No, no land area criteria (small/marginal holder) has been applied. All eligible farmers receive the benefit outright.

**6. Can salaried persons avail this scheme?**
No. Officers and employees of Central and State Government as well as semi-government organizations (except Class IV employees) are ineligible. Also, persons paying Income Tax from non-agricultural income cannot avail this benefit.
