# Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump Yojana 2026

This is a very important government scheme aims to provide farmers with an independent and sustainable irrigation source.

## Key Features of the Scheme

*   For farmers in the general category, a complete set of solar panels and an agricultural pump is provided by paying only **10 percent** of the cost.
*   For farmers belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, the beneficiary's share is only **5 percent**.
*   The remaining amount is provided as a subsidy from the Central and State Governments.
*   Pumps ranging from 3 to 7.5 HP are provided depending on the land area.
*   A five-year repair guarantee, including insurance, is provided.
*   No electricity bills, no worry about load shedding, and electricity supply for irrigation during the day.

## Beneficiary Selection Criteria

*   Farmers with up to 2.5 acres of agricultural land are eligible for solar agricultural pumps with a capacity of up to 3 HP.
*   Farmers with 2.51 to 5 acres of agricultural land are eligible for a 5 HP capacity pump.
*   Farmers with more than 5 acres of agricultural land are eligible for a 7.5 HP solar agricultural pump. (If a pump of lower capacity than the eligible capacity is requested, it will be permissible).
*   Owners of individual or community farm ponds, wells, borewells, as well as farmers with agricultural land next to perennial rivers/streams, are also eligible.
*   A sustainable water source will be verified by MSEDCL. (Pumps cannot be used to lift water from water storage created through water conservation work meant for groundwater recharge).
*   Farmers who have not benefited from Atal Solar Krishi Pump Yojana-1, Atal Solar Krishi Pump Yojana-2, and Mukhyamantri Saur Krushi Pump Yojana will also be eligible.
