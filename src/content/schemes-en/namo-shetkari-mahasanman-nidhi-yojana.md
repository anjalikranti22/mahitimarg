# Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana

The **"Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana" (NSMNY)** is a very important scheme started by the Government of Maharashtra for the income growth of farmers. In addition to the annual ₹6,000 received from the Central Government's PM-KISAN scheme, the State Government provides an additional ₹6,000, making a total of **₹12,000 per year** for eligible farmers.

**Government Resolution No.:** Kisani-2023/C.R. 42/11 A (Date: June 15, 2023)

## Key Benefits

*   An additional annual financial assistance of **₹6,000** from the State Government.
*   This amount is deposited directly into the bank account (DBT) in **three installments** of ₹2,000:
    *   **First Installment:** April — July
    *   **Second Installment:** August — November
    *   **Third Installment:** December — March
*   The amount is credited via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.

## Eligibility Criteria

*   Farmer families (Husband, wife, and minor children) who have cultivable land in their name.
*   Ownership of land is required to be as of **February 1, 2019**.
*   The applicant must be eligible for the **PM-KISAN** scheme.

## Exclusions (Who is ineligible?)

*   Institutional landholders.
*   Former/current constitutional post holders, MPs, MLAs, Mayors, Chairpersons of Zilla Panchayats.
*   Government employees (except MTS/Class IV/Group-D).
*   Pensioners with a monthly pension of ₹10,000 or more.
*   Income tax-paying individuals.
*   Professionals (Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, CAs, Architects).
*   Non-Resident Indians (NRIs).

## Required Documents

*   Aadhaar Card
*   7/12 Extract and 8-A Extract (Land Records)
*   Mutation Records (Ferfar)
*   Ration Card
*   Details of Aadhaar-linked bank account

## How to Apply

1.  **Register on PM-KISAN Portal:** Go to the [PM-KISAN Portal](https://pmkisan.gov.in/) and register yourself.
2.  **Verification by Local Administration:** The application is verified by local officials.
3.  **Approval Process:** Taluka Nodal Officer → District Nodal Officer → State Nodal Officer.
