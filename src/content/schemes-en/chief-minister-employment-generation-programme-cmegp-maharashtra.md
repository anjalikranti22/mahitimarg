The Government of Maharashtra has launched the **'Chief Minister Employment Generation Programme' (CMEGP)**, a new credit-linked subsidy scheme to create employment opportunities by establishing micro and small enterprises in rural and urban areas.

## 1. Scheme Objectives
* Establishing 1,00,000 micro and small enterprises in the next five years and providing employment opportunities to 8-10 lakh youth in the state.
* Preventing migration of rural youth to urban areas and providing self-employment opportunities to traditional artisans and educated unemployed.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

* **Age:** 18 to 45 years (5-year relaxation for special category candidates).
* **Residence:** Applicant must be a resident of Maharashtra.
* **Educational Qualification:**
    * For projects of ₹10 lakh to ₹25 lakh: Minimum 7th pass.
    * For projects above ₹25 lakh: Minimum 10th pass.
* **Family Limit:** Only one person per family can avail the scheme (family means self and spouse).
* **New Projects:** This scheme is applicable only for new enterprises.

## 3. Financial Assistance and Subsidy

| Beneficiary Category | Own Contribution (of project cost) | Subsidy Rate (Urban) | Subsidy Rate (Rural) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Category** | 10% | 15% | 25% |
| **Special Category** (SC/ST/Women/Ex-servicemen/Disabled) | 5% | 25% | 35% |

* **Maximum Project Limit:** ₹50 lakh for manufacturing sector and ₹10 lakh for service or agriculture-based sector.

## 4. Implementation Mechanism

* **Nodal Agency:** Directorate of Industries (DOI), Government of Maharashtra.
* **Implementing Agencies:**
    * Urban areas: District Industries Centre (DIC).
    * Rural areas: Maharashtra State Khadi & Village Industries Board (KVIB).
* For projects exceeding ₹25 lakh, DIC will be the implementing agency for both urban and rural areas.

## 5. Application Process (Brief)
* Applicants must apply only online at [maha-cmegp.gov.in](https://maha-cmegp.gov.in).
* No physical applications will be accepted under any circumstances.
* After bank loan approval, the beneficiary must complete a minimum 10-day Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP) training.

## 6. Important Conditions
* Land cost will not be included in the project cost.
* Rental cost of workspace/stall can be included in the project cost on a 3-year pro-rata basis.
* The subsidy (Margin Money) received from the bank will be kept as a Term Deposit (TDR) for 3 years with no interest charged on it.

## Documents Required for CMEGP

* **Aadhaar Card:** Individual applicants (proprietorship) must have a valid Aadhaar number. For partnerships or registered SHGs, the authorized person must submit their Aadhaar number.
* **Residence Proof:** Applicant must be a Maharashtra resident or possess a Domicile Certificate.
* **Educational Certificate:** Minimum 7th pass for ₹10-25 lakh projects and minimum 10th pass for projects above ₹25 lakh.
* **Caste Certificate:** For availing special category (SC/ST) benefits, a caste certificate from competent authority is required.
* **Disability Certificate:** Disabled applicants must have a valid disability certificate under the 'Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016'.
* **Organization Registration Documents:** For partnerships or SHGs, certified copy of registration must be submitted.
* **Training Certificate:** Applicant should have completed a minimum 10-14 day EDP training. If such training is already completed, its certificate must be attached.

## Additional Details

* **Reservation:** 20% for SC and ST, 30% for women, and 3% for disabled persons from total annual targets.
* **Project Cost Limit:** Maximum ₹50 lakh for manufacturing sector. ₹10 lakh for service sector, agriculture-based, and e-vehicle-based goods transport.
* **Repayment Period:** Loan repayment within 3 to 7 years, with moratorium as decided by the bank.
* **Interest Rate:** Prevailing bank interest rates apply.
* **Credit Guarantee:** Projects approved under this scheme are covered by CGTMSE.
* **Subsidy Lock-in Period:** Subsidy amount (Margin Money) will be kept as Term Deposit for 3 years. After successfully running the business for 3 years and timely loan repayment, it will be adjusted to the loan account.

## CMEGP Online Application Process (Step-by-Step)

**1. Portal Registration:**
* Visit the official portal at https://maha-cmegp.gov.in and register.
* You will receive a User ID and Password to track application status.

**2. Fill Application and Upload Documents:**
* Fill all details accurately in the online form. The portal provides a save feature.
* After completing and uploading required documents, you'll receive a 'Unique Application ID'.

**3. District Level Scrutiny (DLSCC):**
* The District Level Scrutiny and Coordination Sub-Committee will technically review applications.
* The committee prepares a preliminary list of eligible applicants.

**4. Task Force Committee (DLTFC) Approval:**
* The DLTFC, chaired by the District Collector, approves eligible proposals and forwards them to banks with recommendations.

**5. Bank Loan Approval:**
* The bank verifies financial and technical viability and sanctions the loan as per RBI norms.
* After approval, the bank uploads the Sanction Letter on the portal.

**6. EDP Training Completion:**
* After loan sanction, the beneficiary must complete a minimum 10-day EDP training.

**7. Subsidy (Margin Money) Distribution:**
* After training completion, the bank raises a Margin Money demand through the portal.
* After verification, the subsidy amount is deposited as TDR in the beneficiary's bank account.

**8. Lock-in Period:**
* The subsidy remains as TDR for 3 years with no interest charged.
* After successfully operating for 3 years with timely EMI payments, the subsidy is adjusted to the loan account.
