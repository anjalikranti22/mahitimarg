# Swarnima Scheme — For OBC Women

The **Swarnima Scheme** is a loan assistance scheme implemented by the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance, Government of Maharashtra. The main objective of this scheme is to provide self-employment opportunities to **women from Other Backward Classes (OBC)** and help them establish a stable livelihood.

## Key Benefits

*   Loan up to ₹5,00,000 for each **Self-Help Group (SHG) project**.
*   Up to ₹25,000 per member (maximum 20 members per SHG).
*   **Interest Rate:** 5% per annum (concessional rate).
*   **Financial Assistance:** Central Corporation 95% + State Corporation 5% — **Beneficiary Share 0%**.
*   **Repayment Period:** Up to 3 years.

## Eligibility Criteria

*   The applicant must belong to the **Other Backward Class (OBC)** category.
*   For **Women candidates only**.
*   Must be a **Permanent Resident (Domicile)** of Maharashtra state.
*   **Age:** 18 to 50 years.
*   **Income Limit:**
    *   **Rural:** Annual ≤ ₹98,000
    *   **Urban:** Annual ≤ ₹1,20,000

## Required Documents

*   Aadhaar Card
*   Two passport-size photographs (with signature)
*   Residential / Domicile Certificate
*   Caste Certificate — by competent authority
*   Income Certificate
*   Proof of Age (Birth Certificate / Class 10th/12th Marksheet)
*   Bank Account Details (Bank Name, Branch, IFSC Code)

## How to Apply — Offline

1.  Visit the district office of **LIDCOM** or the relevant Other Backward Class Corporation office.
2.  Collect the **Swarnima Scheme Application**.
3.  Fill in personal information and project details.
4.  Attach self-attested documents.
5.  Submit the application and obtain an **acknowledgement receipt**.
