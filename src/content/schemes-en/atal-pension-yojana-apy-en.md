# Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

To provide old-age income security for citizens working in the private or unorganized sector (e.g., daily wagers, farm laborers, shop workers), the Central Government launched the **'Atal Pension Yojana (APY)'**. By investing appropriately, beneficiaries receive a guaranteed pension in their old age.

## 🎯 Eligibility Criteria
* The applicant must be an Indian citizen.
* The age limit to join is between **18 to 40 years**.
* The applicant must have a savings account in a bank or post office.
* *(Note: As per new rules, income taxpayers are no longer eligible to join this scheme.)*

## 💰 Benefits & Subsidy
* Upon completing 60 years of age, a guaranteed monthly pension of **₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000, or ₹5,000** is provided (depending on your contribution).
* **Family Security:** In the event of the pensioner's death, the spouse will continue to receive the same pension for life.
* If both the pensioner and spouse pass away, the accumulated corpus till age 60 is given to the nominee.

## 📋 Procedure & Required Documents
1. **Bank Visit:** Visit the bank or post office where you hold a savings account.
2. **APY Form:** Fill out the 'Atal Pension Yojana' registration form.
3. **Auto-Debit:** The monthly premium is calculated based on your desired pension amount and current age. This premium is automatically deducted (auto-debited) from your account every month.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
**1. If I am 18 years old and want a pension of ₹5,000, how much do I need to pay?**
If you are 18 and opt for a monthly pension of ₹5,000 after age 60, you will only need to contribute ₹210 every month until you turn 60.

**2. Can I exit the scheme prematurely?**
Yes, under certain specific conditions or severe illnesses, you can close the scheme and get your accumulated funds refunded.
