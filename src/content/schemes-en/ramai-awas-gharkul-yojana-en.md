# Ramai Awas Yojana

The State Government of Maharashtra implements the **'Ramai Awas Yojana'** to help homeless citizens or those living in kutcha houses belonging to the Scheduled Castes (SC) and Navabouddha categories build their own permanent (pucca) homes.

## 🎯 Eligibility Criteria
* The applicant must be a resident of Maharashtra and belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) or Navabouddha category.
* Annual family income must not exceed ₹1 lakh in rural areas and ₹3 lakh in urban areas.
* The family must not own a pucca house anywhere in Maharashtra.
* The applicant must have their own land (or the land where their kutcha house stands) to build the new house.

## 💰 Benefits & Subsidy
* **For Rural Areas:** A subsidy of **₹1,20,000** for house construction, plus additional funds for MGNREGA wages and a toilet (totaling around ₹1.5 lakh).
* **For Urban Areas:** In Municipal Corporation and Municipal Council limits, a subsidy of **₹2,50,000** is provided for house construction.

## 📋 Procedure & Required Documents
1. **Application Process:** Application forms are available at the Gram Panchayat office for rural areas, and at the Municipal Council/Corporation offices for urban areas.
2. **Documents:** Caste certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar card, proof of residence (7/12, 8-A, or property tax receipt), and bank passbook.
3. **Approval:** After scrutiny by the Social Welfare Department, the house is approved, and funds are transferred to the bank account in stages based on construction progress.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
**1. Where can I check the Ramai Awas Yojana beneficiary list?**
For rural areas, the approved beneficiary list can be checked at the Gram Panchayat office. For urban areas, check with your Ward Officer or Municipal Council office.

**2. What if I do not own land?**
Citizens without land are provided an additional financial aid of ₹50,000 to purchase land under the government's 'Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay' scheme.
