# Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Shetkari Karj Mafi Yojana 2026 (PAHSKY)

The Government of Maharashtra has made a major announcement for farmers in the 2026-27 State Budget. Under this scheme, **outstanding loans up to ₹2 lakh** of millions of farmers in the state will be waived.

## Required Documents

*   Aadhaar Card (mandatory to be linked with the bank account)
*   Xerox of Bank Passbook
*   7/12 and 8-A Extract
*   Certificate from the society or bank where the loan was taken

## How to Check the Beneficiary List?

Currently, the work of data collection by the government is in progress. Once the entire database is ready, the list will be published on the official portal. As soon as the list is published, we will provide the direct link to view it on this page.

## Eligibility Criteria

*   **Loan Waiver:** Waiver of outstanding crop loans up to ₹2 lakh.
*   **Incentive Subsidy:** A benefit of ₹50,000 for farmers who repay their loans regularly.
*   **Arrears Term:** Outstanding loans until September 30, 2025, will be considered.
