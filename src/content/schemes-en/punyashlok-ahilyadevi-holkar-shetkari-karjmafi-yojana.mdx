# Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana 2026
**Government Resolution (GR) Date:** 02 June 2026

## Scheme Overview
To provide permanent relief to farmers burdened by recurring debt due to natural calamities, the Government of Maharashtra has officially issued the resolution for the **Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana, 2026**. The scheme grants a loan waiver of up to ₹2.00 Lakh to eligible defaulting farmers and an incentive of up to ₹50,000 for farmers who repay their loans regularly.

---

## 1. Loan Waiver Details
* **Eligibility Period:** Short-term crop loans taken between **01 April 2019 and 31 March 2025**.
* **Overdue Criteria:** The loan must be overdue as of **30 September 2025** and remain unpaid up to **31 March 2026**.
* **Maximum Waiver:** Eligible farmers will receive a loan waiver of up to **₹2.00 Lakh** per farmer (irrespective of landholding size).
* **Restructured Loans:** Short-term crop loans that were restructured or rescheduled into medium-term loans are also eligible, subject to the same dates and ₹2.00 Lakh limit.
* **Beneficiaries of the 2019 Scheme:** Farmers who previously benefited from the *Mahatma Jotirao Phule Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana 2019* will receive a maximum waiver of **₹50,000** for loans meeting the above criteria.

## 2. One-Time Settlement (OTS)
* **For Loans Above ₹2 Lakh:** If the total outstanding amount exceeds ₹2.00 Lakh, the farmer must first independently repay the excess amount to the bank. After this repayment, the scheme will provide the ₹2.00 Lakh benefit.
* **For 2019 Beneficiaries:** If the outstanding amount exceeds ₹50,000, the farmer must pay the excess amount first to receive the ₹50,000 benefit.
* **Deadline:** Farmers have until **31 March 2027** to deposit their share (the excess amount).
* **NPA Accounts:** For Non-Performing Asset (NPA) accounts in nationalized, private, and rural banks, a state-level committee will negotiate and determine a discount on the ledger balance.

## 3. Incentive for Prompt Repayment
* **Eligibility:** Farmers who have fully repaid their short-term crop loans on time in **any two of the three financial years (2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25)**.
* **Benefit Amount:** Up to **₹50,000** per farmer.
* **Minimum Benefit:** If the repaid loan amount in 2023-24 or 2024-25 was less than ₹50,000, the farmer will receive the actual amount repaid, or a minimum incentive of **₹5,000**.
* **Conditions:** * Farmers must also repay any loans taken in 2025-26 and 2026-27 on time.
  * A farmer cannot simultaneously receive both the loan waiver and the prompt repayment incentive.

## 4. Mandatory Requirements & Eligible Institutions
* **Institutions Covered:** Loans from Nationalized Banks, Private Banks, Regional Rural Banks, District Central Cooperative Banks, and Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS).
* **Mandatory Registrations:** Aadhaar authentication and registration on the **AgriStack** platform are compulsory.
* **Individual Basis:** The scheme is applicable on a per-individual-farmer basis.

## 5. Ineligible Persons
The following categories are explicitly excluded from the scheme:
1. Current or former Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, and elected members of local bodies (Zilla Parishad, Municipalities, etc.).
2. Central and State Government officers/employees with a combined monthly salary exceeding ₹25,000.
3. Employees/officers of State PSUs (e.g., Mahavitaran, MIDC, ST Corporation) and aided institutions earning over ₹25,000/month.
4. Individuals paying income tax from non-agricultural income sources.
5. Pensioners receiving a monthly pension of more than ₹25,000 (excluding ex-servicemen).
6. Office bearers (Chairmen, Vice-Chairmen, Directors) and employees of APMCs, cooperative sugar factories, spinning mills, cooperative banks, and milk unions earning over ₹25,000/month (Note: Seasonal workers of cooperative sugar factories are eligible).

## 6. Implementation and "Sahakar Stack"
* **Online Process:** The scheme will be implemented 100% online.
* **Cabinet Sub-Committee:** A sub-committee headed by the Hon. Chief Minister has been constituted to review and monitor the scheme.
* **Sahakar Stack:** A new "Co-operative Data Repository for Agricultural Loans" (Sahakar Stack) will be established by the Commissioner of Cooperation to streamline agricultural credit disbursal in the state.

## Eligibility Criteria

*   **Loan Waiver:** Waiver of outstanding crop loans up to ₹2 lakh.
*   **Incentive Subsidy:** A benefit of ₹50,000 for farmers who repay their loans regularly.
*   **Arrears Term:** Outstanding loans until September 30, 2025, will be considered.
