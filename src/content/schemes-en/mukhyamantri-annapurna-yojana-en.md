# Mukhyamantri Annapurna Yojana

To free women from kitchen smoke and provide relief from the high costs of gas cylinders during inflation, the Maharashtra State Government launched the **'Mukhyamantri Annapurna Yojana'**. Under this scheme, eligible families receive 3 LPG gas cylinders completely free of charge annually.

## 🎯 Eligibility Criteria
* **Ladki Bahin Beneficiaries:** Women receiving benefits under the 'Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin' scheme are eligible.
* **Ujjwala Yojana Beneficiaries:** Beneficiaries of the 'Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)' in Maharashtra are also eligible.
* The gas connection must be registered in the name of the woman.
* There is a limit of 3 free cylinders per family (per ration card).

## 💰 Benefits & Subsidy
* Families are provided with 3 free LPG gas cylinders in a financial year (April to March).
* After booking and paying the full amount for the cylinder, the state government deposits the base amount of the cylinder directly into the woman's bank account (DBT) as a subsidy.

## 📋 Procedure & Required Documents
1. **No Separate Application:** There is no need to fill out a separate application form for this scheme.
2. **Data Linking:** Beneficiaries are selected by matching data from the Ladki Bahin scheme and Gas Companies (HP, Indane, Bharat Gas) at the government level.
3. **Rules:** To receive the benefit, the woman's Aadhaar card must be linked to her bank account and gas connection. The woman must also book the cylinder under her own name.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
**1. Do I get all 3 cylinders at once?**
No, as per government rules, the subsidy amount is returned to your account for cylinders booked at specific monthly intervals.

**2. Will I get the benefit if the gas connection is in a man's name?**
No, to avail the Mukhyamantri Annapurna Yojana benefits, the gas connection must strictly be in the name of a female family member.
