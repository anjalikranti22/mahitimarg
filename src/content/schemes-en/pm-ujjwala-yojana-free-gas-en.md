# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

The Central Government launched the **'Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)'** to safeguard the health of women and children in rural and poor households by providing them with a clean cooking fuel. Under this scheme, eligible women receive a free LPG gas connection.

## 🎯 Eligibility Criteria
* The applicant must be a woman.
* The woman must be over 18 years of age.
* The family must belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category (e.g., listed in SECC-2011, SC/ST households, PMAY beneficiaries, etc.).
* There should be no existing LPG connection in the household.

## 💰 Benefits & Subsidy
* A new gas connection is provided completely **free of cost**.
* The cost of the first refill (Gas Cylinder) and the gas stove (Hotplate) is also borne by the government or oil companies.
* The subsidy is deposited directly into the beneficiary's bank account via DBT.

## 📋 Procedure & Required Documents
1. **Application:** Visit your nearest gas agency (HP, Indane, Bharat Gas) to fill out the Ujjwala Yojana form, or apply online via 'pmuy.gov.in'.
2. **Documents:** e-KYC, Ration Card, Aadhaar Card, passport-size photographs, and a copy of the bank passbook.
3. **Verification:** After document verification by the gas agency, the free gas connection is issued.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
**1. Do I have to pay money for the Ujjwala Yojana?**
No, the initial connection and the first stove are provided free of cost by the government. However, customers have to pay for subsequent cylinder refills, on which they receive a government subsidy.

**2. Can I get the Ujjwala scheme if I already have a gas connection?**
No, the benefit of this scheme is only given if there is no existing gas connection in the household (on the ration card).
