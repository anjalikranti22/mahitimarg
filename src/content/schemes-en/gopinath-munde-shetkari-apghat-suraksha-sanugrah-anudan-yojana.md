# Gopinath Munde Farmer Accident Insurance Scheme

The Gopinath Munde Farmer Accident Insurance Scheme is a vital insurance scheme providing **financial assistance to farmer families during unfortunate accident situations** in Maharashtra. In case of accidental death or permanent disability of a farmer, financial aid is provided to the affected family. This scheme operates **24 hours** and is independent of any other insurance scheme.

**Government Resolution No.:** Agriculture/2023/P.C. 84/11-A

## Key Benefits

| Type of Accident | Compensation Amount |
|---|---|
| Accidental Death | ₹2,00,000 |
| Loss of Two Limbs / Two Eyes / One Limb + One Eye | ₹2,00,000 |
| Loss of One Limb or One Eye | ₹1,00,000 |

## Eligibility Criteria

*   **Farmer registered as landholder in Land Records** in Maharashtra.
*   **One family member** who is not a landholder is also eligible.
*   **Age at the time of accident: 10 to 75 years**.

## Exclusions

*   Natural Death.
*   Suicide or attempted suicide.
*   Accidents under the influence of intoxicants/alcohol.
*   Self-inflicted Injuries.
*   Death/disability due to violation of law.
*   Accidents prior to the commencement of the policy.

## Required Documents

*   **Land Records:** 7/12 Extract and 6-D Mutation Entry
*   **Identity/Age Proof:** Aadhaar Card, School Leaving Certificate, or Voter ID
*   **Incident Reports:** FIR, Inquest Panchanama, Spot Panchanama
*   **Medical Reports:** Post-Mortem Report and Viscera Report (if PM is inconclusive)
*   **For Disability:** Disability Certificate (issued by District Surgeon)
*   **For Death:** Official Death Certificate

## How to Apply — Offline

1.  Submit claim with all documents to **Taluka Agriculture Officer** within **30 days** of the accident.
2.  **Joint Investigation Team** (Revenue + Police + Agriculture) submits report to **Tehsildar within 8 days**.
3.  Taluka Agriculture Officer verifies and forwards eligible claims.
4.  **Committee under Tehsildar's chairmanship** decides within **30 days**.
5.  Approved amount is deposited into the bank account via **ECS (Electronic Clearing Service)**.
6.  **Fee: Free of Charge**
