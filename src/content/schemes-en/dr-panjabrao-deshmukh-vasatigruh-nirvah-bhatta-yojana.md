# Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Hostel Maintenance Allowance Scheme

The Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Hostel Maintenance Allowance Scheme is an important educational scheme launched by the Government of Maharashtra for students from economically weaker sections. Students pursuing professional and technical higher education receive a hostel maintenance allowance for **10 months** every academic year.

## Key Benefits

| Category | Mumbai/Pune/Nagpur/Aurangabad | Other Locations |
|---|---|---|
| Family Income ≤ ₹1,00,000 | ₹30,000/year | ₹20,000/year |
| Small Land-holder / Registered Labor | ₹30,000/year | ₹20,000/year |
| Family Income ≤ ₹8,00,000 (Others) | ₹3,000/year | ₹2,000/year |

## Eligibility Criteria

*   Indian citizen, **Domicile of Maharashtra State**.
*   Student admitted to a Professional/Technical course (Diploma/Graduation/PG).
*   Admission through **Centralized Admission Process (CAP)**.
*   Family annual income should not exceed **₹8,00,000**.
*   Maximum **2 children** per family can avail the benefit.
*   Minimum **50% attendance** in the previous session required.
*   No more than **2 years gap** in the course.

## Exclusions

*   Students of Deemed/Private Universities.
*   Students already receiving other scholarships or allowances.

## Required Documents

*   Domicile Certificate
*   HSC & SSC Mark Sheets
*   Parents' Income Certificate
*   Previous Year's Mark Sheet
*   Affidavit
*   Tehsildar's Certificate — for small land-holders / Labor Registration Certificate
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Father's PAN Card (mandatory)
*   Student's and Mother's PAN Card (optional)

## How to Apply

1.  Visit the [MahaDBT Portal](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/).
2.  Click **"New Applicant Registration"** and register.
3.  Login → Click **"Find Eligible Scheme"**.
4.  Select **"Directorate of Medical Education Research" → "Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Hostel Maintenance Allowance"**.
5.  Fill the form and submit.
