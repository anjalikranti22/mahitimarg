# Stand Up India Scheme

The Government of India launched the **'Stand Up India'** scheme to promote entrepreneurship among women and Scheduled Castes/Scheduled Tribes (SC/ST). It provides accessible bank loans for setting up new enterprises with large capital requirements.

## 🎯 Eligibility Criteria
* The applicant must be a woman (of any category) OR a male/female belonging to the SC/ST category.
* Age must be above 18 years.
* The business must be a Greenfield Project (a completely new enterprise in the manufacturing, services, or trading sectors).
* In the case of a non-individual enterprise (company), at least 51% of the shareholding and controlling stake must be held by an SC/ST or woman entrepreneur.

## 💰 Benefits & Subsidy
* Eligible entrepreneurs are provided with a composite bank loan (Working Capital + Term Loan) ranging from **₹10 Lakh to ₹1 Crore**.
* The loan is backed by a credit guarantee through the Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme for Stand Up India (CGSSI).
* The scheme covers up to 85% of the project cost, provided the borrower brings in a minimum of 15% as margin money.

## 📋 Procedure & Required Documents
1. **Online Application:** You can apply directly online through the official portal at 'standupmitra.in'.
2. **Bank Visit:** Applications can also be submitted directly at the nearest nationalized bank branch or through the Lead District Manager.
3. **Documents:** Detailed Project Report, proof of identity, caste certificate (if SC/ST), and necessary business licenses (e.g., Udyam Registration).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
**1. Can I get this loan to expand my existing business?**
No, the Stand Up India scheme is strictly limited to setting up new, first-time ventures (Greenfield Enterprises).

**2. Is collateral required for this loan?**
Typically, banks may ask for collateral. However, if collateral is unavailable, the loan can be secured under the government's 'Credit Guarantee Fund' meant for this scheme.
