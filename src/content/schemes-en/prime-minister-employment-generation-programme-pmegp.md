This is an excellent summary and guide for the **Prime Minister Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)** scheme. The objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to educated unemployed youth and artisans in rural and urban areas to start their own businesses.

Here is the important information about this scheme presented in a simplified manner:

**Prime Minister Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) - Key Points**

This scheme is run by the **Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME)**. At the national level, the **Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC)** is the main nodal agency.

## 1. Main Objectives of the Scheme

*   To generate new employment opportunities in rural and urban areas.
*   To bring together traditional artisans and unemployed youth and motivate them towards self-employment.
*   To stop the migration of youth from rural areas to cities.
*   To increase the income-earning capacity of artisans.

## 2. Project Cost Limits

The maximum limit for projects approved under this scheme is as follows:

*   **Manufacturing Sector:** Up to ₹25 lakh.
*   **Service/Business Sector:** Up to ₹10 lakh.

## 3. Nature of Fund and Subsidy

The subsidy received by beneficiaries depends on their category and sector:

| Beneficiary Category | Own Contribution (of Project Cost) | Subsidy Rate (Urban Area) | Subsidy Rate (Rural Area) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Category** | 10% | 15% | 25% |
| **Special Category** (SC/ST/OBC/Women/Disabled/Ex-servicemen, etc.) | 05% | 25% | 35% |

*Note: The remaining project cost is provided as a loan from banks.*

## 4. Eligibility Conditions

*   **Age:** Minimum 18 years completed.
*   **Education:** For projects above ₹10 lakh (Manufacturing) or ₹5 lakh (Service), the beneficiary must have passed at least the **8th standard**.
*   **Income Limit:** There is no income limit condition.
*   **Project Type:** This scheme is only for **new projects**. It is not for expansion of old units or for units that have already benefited from any other scheme.
*   **Entities:** Besides individuals, Self-Help Groups, registered institutions, and cooperative societies are also eligible.

## 5. Required Documents

1. Caste Certificate (for reserved categories).
2. Educational Qualification Certificate/Marksheet.
3. Project Report (Detailed Project Report - DPR).
4. Aadhaar Card and PAN Card.
5. Population certificate if the unit is in a rural area.
