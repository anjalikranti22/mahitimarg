# Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

## 1. Scheme Overview
The **Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)** is a flagship scheme of the Government of India. This scheme facilitates micro-loans up to ₹20 lakh to micro-enterprises active in the non-corporate, non-farm sector in manufacturing, trading, or service sectors.

*   Includes agriculture-related activities such as poultry, dairy, beekeeping, etc.
*   **Objective:** To provide financial assistance to non-corporate, non-farm micro and small enterprises.

### Lending Institutions
*   Public and Private Sector Banks
*   Regional Rural Banks (RRB) and Cooperative Banks
*   Micro Finance Institutions (MFI) and NBFCs
*   Small Finance Banks (SFB)

---

## 2. Loan Categories and Benefits
Based on the growth stage and funding needs of the beneficiary entrepreneur, the scheme is categorized into four classes:

| Category | Loan Limit |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Shishu** | Up to ₹50,000 |
| **Kishore** | Above ₹50,000 up to ₹5 lakh |
| **Tarun** | Above ₹5 lakh up to ₹10 lakh |
| **Tarun Plus** | Above ₹10 lakh up to ₹20 lakh (for those who have successfully repaid previous loans) |

*   **Interest Rates:** As per the internal guidelines of the banks.
*   **Processing Fees:** Usually, no processing fee is charged for 'Shishu' loans.

---

## 3. Eligibility
Any of the following entities are eligible for this loan:
*   Individuals
*   Proprietorships
*   Partnerships
*   Private Limited Companies

**Conditions:**
1. The applicant should not be a defaulter to any bank.
2. The Credit History should be satisfactory.
3. Must possess the necessary skills/knowledge for the business.

---

## 4. Application Process

### Online Application (Udyamimitra Portal)
1.  Visit the portal [Udyamimitra Portal](https://www.udyamimitra.in).
2.  **Registration:** Click on 'Apply Now' and select New Entrepreneur/Existing Entrepreneur.
3.  **Fill Details:** Perform OTP verification by entering name, email, and mobile number.
4.  **Select Category:** Choose the appropriate option among Mudra Shishu/Kishore/Tarun.
5.  **Upload Documents:** Upload identity proof, address proof, and fill in business information.
6.  **Application Number:** Save the application number received after submission for future reference.

---

## 5. Documents Checklist

### Common Documents (For all categories)
*   **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.
*   **Address Proof:** Electricity bill, telephone bill (must not be older than 2 months).
*   **Photograph:** 2 recent colored photos of the applicant.
*   **Business Proof:** License, registration certificate, or proof of address.

### Additional Documents for Kishore and Tarun Loans
*   Bank statement for the last 6 months.
*   Balance Sheet and IT Returns for the last 2 years (for loans above ₹2 lakh).
*   Projected Balance Sheet.
*   Project Report with details of technical and financial feasibility.
*   Partnership Deed / Memorandum of Association of the company.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. Is a Khadi activity eligible under this scheme?**
Yes, Khadi activities are eligible under Mudra loans.

**2. Is a loan available for purchasing a taxi or tempo?**
Yes, Mudra loans can be obtained for purchasing vehicles for commercial use.

**3. How much time does it take for a Shishu loan?**
If all documents are correct, Shishu loans are processed quickly.

**4. Is an IT return necessary for a ₹20 lakh loan?**
Yes, for large amount loans, banks usually demand IT returns for the last 2 years.

---
**Important Note:** No official agent or middleman has been appointed for Mudra loans. Do not pay money to any individual.
