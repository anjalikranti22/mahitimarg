# MahaDBT Scholarship 2026

The Government of Maharashtra implements various scholarship schemes for students through the MahaDBT (MahaDBT) portal.

## Key Schemes

*   **Post-Matric Scholarship:** For students pursuing education after the 10th standard.
*   **Tuition Fee Reimbursement Scheme:** For professional courses (Engineering, Medical, etc.).
*   **Maintenance Allowance:** For students residing in hostels.

## Required Documents

*   Aadhaar Card
*   Caste Certificate
*   Income Certificate
*   Previous Year's Marksheet
*   Bank Passbook
*   Ration Card
