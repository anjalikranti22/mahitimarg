# Bhausaheb Fundkar Falbag Lagvad Scheme

The Bhausaheb Fundkar Falbag Lagvad Scheme is an important agricultural scheme launched by the Government of Maharashtra from **Kharif season 2018-19**. The main objective of this scheme is to increase the horticulture area through the plantation of **16 perennial fruit crops** and increase farmers' income. Subsidy is provided over three years, with a mandatory survival rate of 80% in the first year and 90% in the second year.

## 16 Crops Covered

Mango, Cashew, Guava, Sapota (Chiku), Custard Apple, Pomegranate, Lemon, Coconut, Tamarind, Fig, Amla (Indian Gooseberry), Kokum, Jackfruit, Jamun (Java Plum), Orange, Sweet Lime (Mosambi)

## Key Benefits

Under this scheme, subsidies are provided through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) for various aspects of fruit plantation:

*   **Pit Digging**
*   **Planting of Grafts/Seedlings**
*   **Chemical & Organic Fertilizers**
*   **Crop Protection & Gap Filling**

## Eligibility Criteria

*   The applicant must be a **permanent resident of Maharashtra**.
*   The applicant must own **cultivable agricultural land**:
    *   **Konkan Division:** 0.10 to 10.00 hectares
    *   **Rest of Maharashtra:** 0.20 to 6.00 hectares
*   **Priority:** Small/marginal farmers, Scheduled Castes/Tribes (SC/ST), women farmers.

## Exclusions

*   Institutional landholders/beneficiaries.

## Required Documents

*   Aadhaar Card
*   7/12 Certificate
*   8-A Certificate
*   Caste Certificate for SC/ST
*   Self-Declaration
*   Pre-Sanction Letter
*   Bills for Implements/Inputs

## How to Apply

1.  Visit the [MahaDBT Portal](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/).
2.  Register or log in.
3.  Go to **"Agriculture"** section → Select **"Bhausaheb Fundkar Falbag Lagvad Scheme"**.
4.  Fill the application, upload required documents, and submit.
5.  Verification is done by local agricultural officers.
